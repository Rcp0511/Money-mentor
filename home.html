Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Money Mentor? We've got answers.

Is Money Mentor free to use? ▼

Yes! Money Mentor is completely free to use. All core learning materials, quizzes, interactive calculators, and progress trackers are accessible without any hidden fees.

Do I need prior financial knowledge to start? ▼

Not at all! Money Mentor is designed for all skill levels—from absolute beginners learning budgeting basics to intermediate learners mastering compound growth and credit score optimization.

Is my personal data saved or shared? ▼

Your privacy is paramount. Money Mentor operates entirely in your browser using local storage for theme choices and quick calculators. No personal banking details are ever requested or stored.

How do I open the main learning app? ▼

Simply click the "Enter Money Mentor App" button anywhere on this page or navigate to Money_Mentor_Updated.html to launch your complete learning dashboard.
